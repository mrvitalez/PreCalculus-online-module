This activity practises every part of Module 1, from intervals and exponents to solving equations graphically; type exact answers such as sqrt(17) or -1/(3x), or decimals where the item asks for them.

Scoring and how to type answers

10 graded items, 1 point each, plus a short reflection. Write your solution in each solution box, then type your final answer. Right after submitting you will see your score and which items are correct or wrong.

  • Number answers: a decimal with at least 3 significant figures (2.236) or an exact value (sqrt(5), 3/8, -7/2, pi/6, ln(20)/ln(5)). log means base 10 and ln base \(e\). Angles in degrees: type only the number of degrees (36.87).
  • Formula answers: type the expression, e.g. (x+3)/(x-1), 2x^2 - 5, sqrt(x-4), cbrt(x+1), 500(1.04)^t, log(x). Use ^ for powers, sqrt( ) for square roots and pi for π. Any correct equivalent form is accepted. The box below the answer shows how your answer was read.
