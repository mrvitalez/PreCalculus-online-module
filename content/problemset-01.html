✎Problems

  1. (1.1-1.2) (a) Let \(A = [-4, 2)\) and \(B = (0, 6]\). Find \(A \cup B\) and \(A \cap B\), and graph both on a number line. (b) Simplify \((x^2y^{-3})^{-2}(xy^2)^3\), using positive exponents only. (c) Rationalize the denominator: \(\dfrac{6}{\sqrt5 - \sqrt2}\).

  2. (1.3) Factor completely: (a) \(x^4 - 13x^2 + 36\); (b) \(2x^3 + 3x^2 - 8x - 12\); (c) \(x^{5/2} - 4x^{1/2}\).

  3. (1.4) (a) Simplify \(\dfrac{x + 2}{x^2 - 1} - \dfrac{1}{x^2 - x}\) and state the excluded values. (b) Rationalize the numerator of \(\dfrac{\sqrt{x + h} - \sqrt{x}}{h}\) and simplify.

  4. (1.5) Solve: (a) \(\dfrac{3}{x - 2} + \dfrac{1}{x + 2} = \dfrac{4}{x^2 - 4}\); (b) \(x^4 - 5x^2 + 4 = 0\); (c) \(3x^2 + 6x - 2 = 0\) by completing the square. (d) Find all values of \(k\) for which \(x^2 + kx + 9 = 0\) has exactly one real solution.

  5. (1.5) A student invests ₱50,000 of her savings, part in a time deposit paying 4% simple interest per year and the rest in bonds paying 6% per year. After one year she earns ₱2,600 in interest. How much did she put in each? Show the equation you used.

  6. (1.6-1.7) (a) Compute \((1 + i)^8\). (b) Find all solutions of \(2x^2 + 2x + 5 = 0\). (c) Solve \(x^3 - 4x \ge 0\) with a sign chart. (d) Solve \(\dfrac{x + 1}{x - 3} \lt 2\).

  7. (1.8-1.9) The points \(A(-1, 3)\), \(B(3, 11)\) and \(C(5, 0)\) are the vertices of a triangle. (a) Use slopes to show that the triangle has a right angle at \(A\). (b) Find its area. (c) Find the equation of the circle that passes through \(A\), \(B\) and \(C\). (Hint: in a right triangle, the hypotenuse is a diameter of that circle.) (d) Write the equation of the line through \(A\) parallel to \(BC\).

  8. (1.10) A model rocket launched from a 1.5 m platform has height \(h(t) = -4.9t^2 + 39.2t + 1.5\) metres after \(t\) seconds. (a) Graph \(h\) and the line \(y = 50\) on the same axes, and use the graph to estimate (to 2 decimal places) the time interval during which the rocket is at least 50 m high. (b) Confirm your answer algebraically with the quadratic formula. (c) Estimate when the rocket hits the ground.

  9. (2.1) Let \(f(x) = 1 - 4x^2\). Find \(f(-a)\), \(f(a + h)\) and the difference quotient \(\dfrac{f(a + h) - f(a)}{h}\), \(h \ne 0\). Then find the domain of \(g(x) = \dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{x^2 - 9}\).

  10. (2.2) Sketch the graph of the piecewise function \(f(x) = \begin{cases} x + 2 & x \lt 0 \\ 4 - x^2 & x \ge 0 \end{cases}\). Use solid and open dots correctly, and state the domain and range. Does the graph pass the Vertical Line Test?

  11. (2.3) Graph \(f(x) = x^4 - 8x^2\) in Desmos. Find (a) the zeros, (b) all local maximum and minimum values and where they occur, (c) the intervals on which \(f\) is increasing and decreasing, (d) whether \(f\) is even, odd or neither.

  12. (2.4) A water tank on the roof of an RTU building is drained. The volume left after \(t\) minutes is \(V(t) = 500\left(1 - \dfrac{t}{40}\right)^2\) litres, \(0 \le t \le 40\). Find the average rate of change of \(V\) between \(t = 0\) and \(t = 10\), and between \(t = 10\) and \(t = 20\). Interpret the two answers.

  13. (2.5) Water freezes at 0 °C = 32 °F and boils at 100 °C = 212 °F. (a) Express the Fahrenheit temperature \(F\) as a linear function of the Celsius temperature \(C\). (b) What does the slope mean? (c) Convert a normal body temperature of 37 °C to °F. (d) At what temperature are the two readings equal?

  14. (2.6) Explain how the graph of \(g(x) = -\tfrac12|x - 4| + 3\) is obtained from the graph of \(y = |x|\). Give the vertex, the range, and the intervals where \(g\) is increasing and decreasing. Is \(g\) even, odd or neither?

  15. (2.7) Let \(f(x) = \sqrt{x + 1}\) and \(g(x) = x^2 - 2x\). Find \(f \circ g\) and \(g \circ f\), and the domain of each. Simplify \(f \circ g\) as far as possible.

  16. (2.7) Let \(f(x) = \sqrt[3]{2x - 5} + 1\). Explain why \(f\) is one-to-one, find \(f^{-1}(x)\), and verify that \(f\big(f^{-1}(x)\big) = x\). Then compute \(f^{-1}(3)\).
