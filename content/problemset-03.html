✎Problems

  1. (5.1) Find the reference number and the terminal point determined by (a) \(t = \dfrac{17\pi}{6}\) and (b) \(t = -\dfrac{7\pi}{3}\).

  2. (5.1) The terminal point of \(t\) is \(P(-0.6, 0.8)\). First verify that \(P\) is on the unit circle. Then find the terminal points determined by \(-t\), \(\pi - t\) and \(t + \pi\). Explain each using symmetry of the circle.

  3. (5.2) If \(\tan t = -\dfrac34\) and \(\cos t \gt 0\), find the values of the other five trigonometric functions of \(t\).

  4. (5.2) Determine whether each function is even, odd or neither: (a) \(f(t) = t\sin t\) (b) \(g(t) = \sin t + \cos t\) (c) \(h(t) = t^2\tan t\). Then state the domain of \(h\).

  5. (5.3) For \(y = 2\sin(\pi x - \tfrac{\pi}{2}) + 1\), find the amplitude, period, phase shift, the interval of one complete period, and the range. Sketch one period.

  6. (5.3) Find the period and the vertical asymptotes of (a) \(y = \cot\dfrac{x}{2}\) and (b) \(y = \sec 2x\), and sketch one period of each.

  7. (5.4) Find the exact value, or explain why it is undefined: (a) \(\cos^{-1}\left(\cos\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\right)\) (b) \(\tan(\tan^{-1} 7)\) (c) \(\sin\left(\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac{5}{13}\right)\right)\) (d) \(\sin^{-1}(\pi/2)\).

  8. (5.5) The water depth at a pier on Manila Bay varies between 2.3 m at low tide and 3.5 m at high tide. High tide occurs at 4:00 AM, and the tide is diurnal with a period of 24.8 hours. (a) Write a model \(D(t) = a\cos(\omega(t - c)) + d\), with \(t\) in hours after midnight. (b) Find the depth at 12:00 noon. (c) A related problem: a damped spring moves by \(y = 10e^{-0.5t}\cos 4\pi t\) (cm). Find its frequency and the time when its amplitude first falls below 1 cm.

  9. (6.1) A sector of a circle of radius 9 cm has a central angle of \(140°\). Find (a) the length of its arc and (b) its area, exactly and to two decimal places.

  10. (6.1) A bicycle wheel has a diameter of 66 cm and turns at 180 revolutions per minute. Find (a) its angular speed in rad/min and (b) the linear speed of the bicycle in m/min and in km/h.

  11. (6.2) Solve the right triangle \(ABC\) with \(C = 90°\), hypotenuse \(c = 20\) and \(A = 28°\). Round sides to two decimal places.

  12. (6.3) Without a calculator, find the exact values of (a) \(\sin 300°\), (b) \(\cos(-135°)\), (c) \(\tan\dfrac{7\pi}{6}\), (d) \(\cot 120°\).

  13. (6.3) (a) Find the area of a triangle with sides 12 m and 15 m and included angle \(110°\). (b) If \(\tan\theta = -\dfrac{8}{15}\) and \(\sin\theta \gt 0\), find the other five trigonometric functions of \(\theta\).

  14. (6.4) (a) Find the exact value of \(\cos(\tan^{-1}2)\). (b) Write \(\sin(\tan^{-1}x)\) as an algebraic expression in \(x\). (c) Explain why \(\sin^{-1}(\sin 150°) \ne 150°\).

  15. (6.5) Decide how many triangles satisfy the data, and solve them: (a) \(A = 50°\), \(a = 9\), \(b = 14\); (b) \(A = 42°\), \(a = 15\), \(b = 12\).

  16. (6.6) From Earth, star \(A\) is 4.2 light-years away and star \(B\) is 6.0 light-years away, and the angle between their directions in the sky is \(40°\). (a) Find the distance between the two stars. (b) Use Heron's Formula to find the area of the triangle formed by Earth and the two stars, and check it with \(\tfrac12 ab\sin C\).
