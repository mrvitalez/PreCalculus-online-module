✎Problems

  1. (3.1) Let \(g(x) = 3x^2 + 12x + 5\). Write \(g\) in standard form, find its vertex and its maximum or minimum value, and find its \(x\)- and \(y\)-intercepts. Sketch the graph.

  2. (3.1) A stone is thrown straight up from the roof of a 24.5 m building in Manila. Its height after \(t\) seconds is \(h(t) = -4.9t^2 + 19.6t + 24.5\) metres. (a) When does it reach its maximum height, and what is that height? (b) When does it hit the ground?

  3. (3.2) Sketch \(P(x) = x(x - 2)^2(x + 3)\). State the degree, the end behaviour, the zeros with their multiplicities, and whether the graph crosses or touches at each zero.

  4. (3.3) Use long division to divide \(x^4 - 2x^3 + 3x - 4\) by \(x^2 + x - 1\). Write the answer in the form \(P(x) = D(x)Q(x) + R(x)\).

  5. (3.4) Let \(P(x) = 2x^4 - x^3 - 8x^2 + x + 6\). (a) List all possible rational zeros. (b) Use Descartes' Rule of Signs to give the possible numbers of positive and negative zeros. (c) Find all the zeros and factor \(P\) completely. (d) Show that 3 is an upper bound for the zeros.

  6. (3.5) Find a polynomial \(P(x)\) of degree 3 with real coefficients that has zeros \(-1\) and \(2 - 3i\) and satisfies \(P(0) = 26\). Write its complete factorization and its expanded form.

  7. (3.6) Graph \(r(x) = \dfrac{x^2 - x - 6}{x^2 + x - 2}\). Find any holes, the vertical and horizontal asymptotes, and the intercepts.

  8. (3.7) A rectangular mural for the RTU Earth and Space Sciences building must be 3 m longer than it is high. The area must be at least 40 m² and at most 70 m². Set up and solve the inequalities to find the possible heights.

  9. (4.1) Let \(f(x) = 3 - 2^{x+1}\). Describe how its graph is obtained from \(y = 2^x\), sketch it, and state its domain, range, horizontal asymptote, \(y\)-intercept and \(x\)-intercept (exact and to three decimal places).

  10. (4.2) A cooperative in Marikina offers two savings plans for 100 000 pesos kept for 10 years: Plan A pays 5.5% a year compounded monthly; Plan B pays 5.4% a year compounded continuously. Which plan gives more money, and by how much?

  11. (4.3) (a) Evaluate without a calculator: \(\log_4 64\), \(\log_{25} 5\), \(\log 0.0001\), \(e^{\ln 7}\), \(10^{\log 3 + \log 2}\). (b) Find the domain of \(g(x) = \ln(8 - 2x)\) and the domain, vertical asymptote and \(x\)-intercept of \(h(x) = \log_5(x + 4) - 1\).

  12. (4.4) (a) Expand \(\ln\left(\dfrac{x^3(x - 2)}{\sqrt{x^2 + 1}}\right)\). (b) Write \(3\log a - \tfrac12\log b + 2\log(c + 1)\) as a single logarithm. (c) Use the Change of Base Formula to find \(\log_3 200\) to four decimal places, and check your answer.

  13. (4.5) Solve each equation. Give exact answers, and decimals where needed. (a) \(3^x + 3^{x+2} = 270\) (b) \(8^x = 4^{x+3}\) (c) \(e^{2x} - 5e^x + 6 = 0\) (d) \(\log_6 x + \log_6(x - 5) = 2\).

  14. (4.6) A culture of bacteria has 500 cells at \(t = 0\) and 4000 cells 6 hours later. Assume exponential growth \(n(t) = n_0e^{rt}\). (a) Find \(r\) and the doubling time. (b) When will the culture reach 1 000 000 cells?

  15. (4.6) Radium-226 has a half-life of 1600 years. A laboratory has a 50 mg sample. (a) Write the model \(m(t) = m_0e^{-rt}\). (b) How much remains after 500 years? (c) How long until only 10 mg remains?

  16. (4.7) (a) The Sun has apparent magnitude \(-26.74\) and the full Moon \(-12.7\). How many times brighter does the Sun appear? (b) A star has absolute magnitude \(M = 1.4\) and apparent magnitude \(m = 6.4\). Use the distance modulus \(m - M = 5\log\dfrac{d}{10\text{ pc}}\) to find its distance in parsecs. (c) How many times larger are the waves of a magnitude 7.8 earthquake than those of a magnitude 6.5 earthquake?
